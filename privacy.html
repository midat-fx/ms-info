Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026.

Midat Studio is a private tool. Its only user is its owner, who authorizes access to his own Google accounts and YouTube channels.

What data is accessed

With the owner's consent, the tool reads YouTube channel information and YouTube Analytics reports, and uploads videos to the owner's own channels (scopes: youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly, youtube.upload).

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to show the owner statistics about his own channels and to publish his own videos. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.
